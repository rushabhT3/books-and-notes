# 🎯 THE COMPLETE JLPT N5 MASTERY GUIDE

## A. ASSESSMENT: What N5 Actually Tests

| Component | Requirement | Reality Check |
|-----------|-------------|---------------|
| **Kanji** | 100-120 characters | You need instant recognition, not just memorization |
| **Vocabulary** | 800-1,000 words | 30% of failures come from weak vocab  |
| **Grammar** | ~100 patterns | Must understand in context, not isolation |
| **Listening** | 19/60 minimum | Slow speech, but traps in numbers/times |
| **Reading** | Combined with grammar (38/120) | Speed matters — 40 minutes for all sections |
| **Passing Score** | 80/180 total | Must pass BOTH sections individually |

**Critical insight:** N5 uses **scaled scoring** (IRT), not raw percentages. Getting easy questions wrong hurts more than hard ones .

---

## B. BUILDING BLOCKS: Kana Foundation (Weeks 1-2)

### Hiragana (46 characters)
Master these until you can read them in **under 1 second** each:
- あいうえお → かきくけこ → さしすせそ → たちつてと → なにぬねの → はひふへほ → まみむめも → やゆよ → らりるれろ → わをん

### Katakana (46 characters)
Same speed requirement. Watch for lookalikes: シ(shi) vs ツ(tsu), ソ(so) vs ン(n) 

**Daily drill:** Write each character 20 times while saying it aloud. Use **Tofugu's Kana Guide** or **Dr. Moku's apps**.

## G. GRAMMAR & READING SECTION STRATEGIES

### Reading Comprehension Tactics 

**1. Skimming Strategy**
- Read the question FIRST
- Skim for keywords (who, what, when, where)
- Don't translate every word — get the gist

**2. Time Management**
- Short passages (80 chars): 90 seconds max
- Medium passages (150 chars): 2 minutes max
- Long passages (250 chars): 3 minutes max

**3. Question Types**
- **Main idea:** Look at first and last sentences
- **Detail questions:** Scan for numbers, times, names
- **Inference:** What's implied but not stated?

### Listening Section Mastery  

**Task-Based Comprehension (7 questions)**
- Listen for: Purpose, location, next action
- Trap: Distractors with similar sounding words

**Key Point Comprehension (6 questions)**
- Catch: Numbers, times, prices, dates
- Strategy: Write down numbers immediately

**Quick Response (6 questions)**
- Pattern: Question → immediate answer
- Practice: Shadow daily conversations

**Picture Matching (5 questions)**
- Visualize the scene described
- Eliminate impossible options first

**Daily Listening Routine:**
1. **Shadowing** (15 min): Repeat audio immediately
2. **Dictation** (10 min): Write what you hear
3. **Passive listening** (30 min): Background Japanese

## H. HIDDEN TIPS & EXAM STRATEGIES

### Test-Day Strategies 

1. **The "Daily 15-15-15" Rule:** 15 min kanji, 15 min grammar, 15 min listening daily
2. **Don't skip writing practice:** Muscle memory helps recognition 
3. **Learn grammar through sentences, not rules**
4. **Create an error log:** Track every mistake
5. **Practice speed recognition:** Timed drills weekly

### Common Traps to Avoid

| Trap | Why It Gets You | Solution |
|------|----------------|----------|
| Similar katakana | シ/ツ, ソ/ン | Practice distinguishing daily |
| Time confusion | 4時 vs 7時 (shi vs shichi) | Listen for context |
| Particle tricks | は vs が | Focus on new information |
| Negative questions | ～じゃありませんか | Answer based on facts, not grammar |
| Listening numbers | 4 (し/よん), 7 (しち/なな) | Write immediately |
